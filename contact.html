Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I save destinations and create a custom itinerary?

Click the "Save" or "⭐ Save as Favorite" button on any destination, activity, cultural festival, or cuisine card. You can view, manage, and export your complete itinerary anytime by clicking the 💖 Saved button in the navbar.

Are tour booking confirmations stored on my device?

Yes! When you book a tour or pass, an instant booking voucher with a unique confirmation code (e.g. ETW-XXXXXX) is saved to your account/browser storage. You can view all vouchers by clicking your User menu in the navbar.

Can I use Explore the World without logging in?

Absolutely! You can browse the entire website, use the currency converter, look up travel tips, and plan trips freely as a guest.
