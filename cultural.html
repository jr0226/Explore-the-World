Diwali - Festival of Lights (India)

Diwali is celebrated across India with fireworks, sweets, prayers, and lighting oil lamps. It signifies the spiritual victory of light over darkness and good over evil.

When: October - November (dates vary by lunar calendar)

Where: Throughout India, especially in Delhi, Mumbai, and Varanasi

Highlights: Lighting clay lamps (diyas), bursting fireworks, rangoli designs, and festive family feasts.

Chinese New Year (China & Global Diaspora)

Also known as the Spring Festival, this magnificent celebration marks the beginning of a new year on the traditional lunisolar Chinese calendar.

When: January - February

Where: China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, Malaysia, and Chinatowns worldwide

Highlights: Dragon and lion dances, red lanterns, reunion dinners, red envelopes (hongbao), and firecrackers.

Rio Carnival (Rio de Janeiro, Brazil)

Considered the biggest carnival in the world, Rio Carnival is filled with revelers, floats, and adornments from numerous samba schools.

When: February - March (before Lent)

Where: Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (Sambadrome Marques de Sapucai)

Highlights: Vibrant samba parades, elaborate costumes, street band parties (blocos), and nonstop music.

Oktoberfest (Munich, Germany)

Oktoberfest is the world's largest Volksfest, featuring a beer festival and a traveling funfair held annually in Munich, Bavaria, Germany.

When: Mid-September to the first Sunday in October

Where: Theresienwiese, Munich, Germany

Highlights: Traditional Bavarian attire (Dirndl and Lederhosen), brass folk music, giant pretzels, and festive beer halls.
