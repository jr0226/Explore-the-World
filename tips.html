Expert Travel Wisdom & Guidelines

Always visit the local tourism information center

  • Tourism information centers know everything happening in town.
  • They will guide you to free activities and authentic local special events.
  • They often offer exclusive discounts on activities and transportation passes.

Carry a basic first-aid kit

  • Unexpected injuries happen during travel and local pharmacies or clinics may be closed.
  • Pack essential remedies for stomach upsets, headaches, fevers, or allergies.
  • Check recommended travel vaccinations well ahead of visiting other countries.

Try authentic local food

  • Discover authentic flavors from popular street food stalls and local markets.
  • Every country's ingredients and spices tell a vivid cultural and historical story.
  • Ask friendly locals what they recommend eating for breakfast or dinner!

Cultivate an open, relaxed mindset

  • A great journey is about unforgettable experiences and open-hearted memories.
  • Embrace travel surprises with a smile; they often make the best stories later.
  • Disconnect from daily stress and truly immerse yourself in the present moment.
